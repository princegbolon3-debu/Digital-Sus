How to Install the App

Android phones have built-in security that can block downloaded apps. Follow these simple steps:

⭐ Fix Chrome "This app cannot be installed":

If Chrome shows "This app cannot be installed", do this simple 10-second fix to add it straight to your phone's home screen:

1
Tap Chrome's 3 vertical dots (⋮) in the top right corner.
2
Scroll down the menu and tap "Add to Home screen" (or "Install to device").
3
Tap "Add". The Digital Susu app icon appears immediately on your phone like any Play Store app!
📦 Method 2: If the APK says "App Not Installed"
⚠️ Issue: "Blocked by Play Protect" / "Unsafe app"

Google Play Protect alerts whenever an app is downloaded from the internet. DO NOT tap "OK" (that cancels installation). Instead, tap "More details", then tap "Install anyway".

⚠️ Issue: "Phone not allowed to install unknown apps"

Tap Settings on the message, turn ON the switch next to "Allow from this source", then tap the Back button and tap Install.

⚠️ Issue: "App not installed as package is invalid"

If you previously installed an older test version of Digital Susu on your phone, Android blocks updating it. Uninstall / delete the old app from your phone first, then tap the downloaded APK file.
